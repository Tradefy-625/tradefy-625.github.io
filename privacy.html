Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Yt uploader is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The app requests only the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). It does not read, collect, or store any other Google user data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's private computer and is never shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.

Use of Google data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
